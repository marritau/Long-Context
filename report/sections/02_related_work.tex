\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Long-context evaluation.}
Lost in the Middle motivates position-aware ordering by showing that models can
underuse evidence placed in the middle of long contexts \cite{liu2023lost}.
LongBench, RULER, and NoLiMa provide complementary benchmark settings for
long-context understanding, synthetic control, and low lexical overlap
\cite{bai2023longbench,hsieh2024ruler,modarressi2025nolima}.

\paragraph{Context compression.}
Selective Context, LLMLingua, and LongLLMLingua show that prompt compression can
reduce inference cost while preserving task quality
\cite{li2023selective,jiang2023llmlingua,jiang2024longllmlingua}.

\paragraph{RAG and long context.}
Retrieval-augmented generation and full long-context prompting have different
quality-cost tradeoffs. Routing between them motivates the cost-aware baselines
used in this project \cite{li2024raglongcontext}.

\paragraph{Context extension.}
YaRN, LongRoPE, and LongLoRA improve or adapt positional mechanisms for longer
windows \cite{peng2023yarn,ding2024longrope,chen2023longlora}. PEARL is
orthogonal: it does not change model weights.
